## Automatic smelter — final prompt

> Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: bitmap illustration for a black-and-white Korean mock examination about real Minecraft automation. Create a high-resolution landscape 3:2 image, approximately 1536 by 1024, on a pure white background. A single compact Minecraft Java Edition automatic smelting module, shown clearly in an orthographic isometric cutaway: an ordinary wooden chest at the top, a gray hopper directly beneath that chest feeding the top of a stone furnace, a second gray hopper directly below the furnace pointing sideways into a lower wooden output chest. The furnace front shows the recognizable dark furnace opening with a pale glow. Only these five components, sitting on a small 3 by 3 stone-block platform. Vanilla Minecraft cube geometry and crisp pixelated block textures; monochrome grayscale ink, visible dithering, high contrast suitable for photocopying, no color. Keep the chest lids, hopper funnels and furnace front distinguishable; a clean diagram-like voxel rendering with abundant white space and no dramatic lighting. No people, UI, item counts, text, labels, arrows, logos, watermarks, landscaping or extra machines. The picture is a machine illustration, not a circuit timing diagram.

## Brewing batch — final prompt

> Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: second grayscale bitmap for a Korean Minecraft examination. Make a landscape 3:2 illustration on pure white, a close orthographic isometric view of a vanilla Minecraft brewing stand on one cobblestone pedestal. A recognizable low gray base with a central slender blaze-colored pole rendered in gray and its three bottle-holder arms. Exactly THREE glass water bottles on the stand, each with a dark pixel outline, square shoulders, small neck and pale gray water in the lower half. Beside the stand place ONE isolated pixel-art nether wart ingredient and ONE isolated small blaze powder pile, visually separate on the white background. Vanilla pixelated voxel textures, crisp edges, monochrome high contrast photocopy-friendly scientific textbook illustration. All elements fully visible with generous margin. No GUI, no text, no counts, no labels, no arrows, no people, no extra bottles, no foliage, no color or watermark. The three bottles should be distinctly visible as the three-item brewing batch.
